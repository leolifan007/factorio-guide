Short answer: pack for cold, not for production. Aquilo has almost no local resources, so your rocket is your supply line. The absolute minimum is 4-6 heating towers, 2000+ rocket fuel, 10-15 rocket turrets with 1000 rounds, and 200+ ice platforms. The three things players forget: heat pipes (you cannot build the grid without them), concrete or ice platform for every single tile (nothing builds on snow), and spare heating towers (one failure cascades into a total freeze). Read the full manifest table below before you launch.

What Actually Makes Aquilo Hard

Aquilo is not hard because of enemies. It is hard because of two rules:

Rule Consequence
No local resources Everything except frozen ammonia must be imported
Everything freezes Any building outside heat range stops working

That means your cargo rocket manifest is not a suggestion - it is your entire base. Miss one item and you fly back, which costs a full round trip.

Resource Local on Aquilo?
Frozen ammonia Yes (limited)
Ice Yes (byproduct of brine separation)
Stone No
Copper / Iron No
Coal / Oil No
Water No (must melt ice)

The Three Things Players Forget

These are the items that end runs. All three are cheap - missing them is expensive.

1. Heat pipes

You cannot build a heat grid without them, and Aquilo does not produce them. If you arrive with heating towers but no pipes, you have heat sources you cannot connect. Pack 200+ even for a small base.

2. Surface for every tile

Nothing builds on Aquilo snow. You need ice platform or concrete for literally every tile you place, including under belts and power poles. This is the single most common resource shortfall.

Surface Cost When to Use
Ice platform Ice platform Higher Early, small areas
Concrete Lower Large bases, once you have supply

3. Spare heating towers

If one heating tower runs out of fuel, the buildings in its radius freeze, stop working, and stop consuming fuel - which can starve the next tower. One failure cascades into a total base freeze. Always pack 1-2 spares.

How Much Fuel Is Actually Enough

Rocket fuel is your limiting resource. It fuels the heating towers and is the input for several Aquilo recipes.

Base Scale Rocket Fuel Needed Notes
Scout landing 1000 Enough to test the surface
Starter base 2000-3000 Heat grid + first science
Full base 5000+ Requires a Nauvis resupply rocket

Do not land with under 1000. Running out of fuel on Aquilo means your heating towers stop, your base freezes, and you cannot fix it without a return trip.

Do NOT Bring These

Item Why to Leave It
Pollution-heavy builds No biters on Aquilo - pollution is wasted
Mining equipment Nothing to mine locally
Offshore pumps No liquid water to pump
Large ore buffers No ore to buffer

Aquilo has no biter threat. Skip everything you would bring for defense against evolution - rocket turrets are purely for strafers, the flying enemy unique to this planet.

Pre-Launch Checklist

Before you hit launch, confirm:

Check Confirm
Heating towers + spares 6+ packed
Heat pipes 200+ packed
Fuel 2000+ packed
Ice platform / concrete 200+ / 500+
Rocket turrets + ammo 10+ / 1000+
Chemical plants 10+
Platform can survive the route Uncommon quality or better
Previous planet sciences running All 4 complete

The last row matters most. Do not visit Aquilo until the other planets are producing consistently - you cannot build Aquilo infrastructure without their unlocks.
